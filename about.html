How Root Domain Access Is Different

We designed our academy to bridge the massive gap between academic training and enterprise hiring standards.

Mechanism-Level Depth

We don't stop at CLI syntax. When an OSPF adjacency forms, we inspect the Hello/DBD/LSR/LSU packets in Wireshark. When an Entra ID token is issued, we decode the JWT claims and test conditional access grant conditions.

The Homelab Advantage

Every student learns how to stand up their own self-hosted homelab on Linux using Docker, ZeroTier VPN, and open-source infrastructure. You graduate with a living, running portfolio of services on your resume.

Real Interview Scenarios

Technical interviewers don't ask for definitions; they give you a broken topology and say "fix it." Our mock interviews simulate high-pressure troubleshooting scenarios so you stand out in hiring loops.
