Curriculum Blueprint (5 Official Domains)

Complete breakdown of all 5 domains required for the ISC2 CC examination.

Domain 1 · 26% of Exam

Security Principles

  • ▹ CIA Triad (Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability) and IAAA (Identification, Authentication, Authorization, Accountability)
  • ▹ Risk Management: Threat vs Vulnerability vs Risk, Risk formulas (`Risk = Threat x Vulnerability x Asset Value`)
  • ▹ Quantitative calculations: Asset Value (AV), Exposure Factor (EF), Single Loss Expectancy (`SLE = AV x EF`), Annualized Rate of Occurrence (ARO), Annualized Loss Expectancy (`ALE = SLE x ARO`)
  • ▹ Risk Treatment: Acceptance, Avoidance, Mitigation, Transfer (Insurance)
  • ▹ Regulatory frameworks & privacy laws: GDPR, HIPAA, PCI-DSS, NIST Cybersecurity Framework
Domain 2 · 10% of Exam

Business Continuity (BC), DR & IR

  • ▹ Business Impact Analysis (BIA), Maximum Tolerable Downtime (MTD), RTO & RPO metrics
  • ▹ Backup strategies: Full, Differential (cumulative), and Incremental (non-cumulative)
  • ▹ Alternate site tiers: Redundant/Mirror Site, Hot Site, Warm Site, Cold Site, Mobile Site
  • ▹ Incident Response Phases (NIST SP 800-61): Preparation, Detection & Analysis, Containment/Eradication/Recovery, Post-Incident Activity
Domain 3 · 22% of Exam

Access Controls Concepts

  • ▹ Physical vs Technical (Logical) vs Administrative Controls
  • ▹ Access Control Models: Discretionary (DAC), Mandatory (MAC / Security labels), Role-Based (RBAC), Attribute-Based (ABAC)
  • ▹ Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) factors: Something you know, have, are, somewhere you are, something you do
  • ▹ Privileged Access Management (PAM), Separation of Duties (SoD) & Principle of Least Privilege
  • ▹ SSO & Federation protocols: SAML 2.0, OpenID Connect, OAuth 2.0, Kerberos
Domain 4 & 5 · 42% of Exam

Network Security & Security Operations

  • ▹ OSI & TCP/IP Models, port numbers, protocols (DNS, DHCP, HTTP/HTTPS, SSH, TLS)
  • ▹ Network defense: Firewalls, IDS/IPS, DMZ, Network Segmentation, Zero Trust Architecture
  • ▹ Common network threats: MITM, DDoS, ARP Spoofing, DNS poisoning, Phishing, Ransomware
  • ▹ Data security lifecycle: Create, Store, Use, Share, Archive, Destroy
  • ▹ Security Operations: Patch management, Endpoint protection (EDR), Log management (SIEM), Change management
